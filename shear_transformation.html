Control the shear factor (k)

Shear factor: 1.0

Geometric interpretation

Horizontal shear matrix:

S = [ 1   k ]
    [ 0   1 ]

x' = x + ky
y' = y

The bottom edge remains fixed because its points have y = 0. The top edge moves horizontally because its points have y = 2.

Important: The area remains unchanged. The determinant is:

det(S) = (1 × 1) - (0 × k) = 1
