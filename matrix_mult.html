What should students observe?

Step 1: The original vector is X = (1,1).

Step 2: B doubles x: (1,1) becomes (2,1).

Step 3: A rotates the new vector: (2,1) becomes (-1,2).

Step 4: C = AB is applied directly to the original vector. It also produces (-1,2).

Key mathematical idea: Matrix multiplication is associative: (AB)X = A(BX). The rightmost matrix acts first.
