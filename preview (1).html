Step-by-step mathematical calculation

Notice that B acts first because it is closest to X. Then A acts on the result of B. Matrix multiplication combines these two operations into C.

Important concept for students

Associative property: (AB)X = A(BX). You can combine the matrices first or apply them one by one. The final answer remains the same.

But remember: AB is generally not equal to BA. The order of transformations matters. In AB, B acts first and A acts second.
