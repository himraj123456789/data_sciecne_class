📐 Mathematical calculation

🧠 The most important observation

1. Before collapse Square has area greater than zero. Different points have different outputs.
2. At determinant = 0 Square becomes a line. Area becomes zero. Some different points overlap.
3. Inverse impossible We cannot uniquely determine the original point from the transformed point.

🔍 The information-loss experiment

Consider two different points:

P = (1, 0)      Q = (0, 1)
A × P = (1, 1)
A × Q = (1, 1)
Both points have the same transformed position!

🎓 Remember this relationship

det(A) ≠ 0   →   Inverse exists
det(A) = 0   →   Inverse does not exist

A transformation with nonzero determinant preserves the ability to recover the original point. A zero determinant means at least one dimension of information is lost.
